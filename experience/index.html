    ResearchIndustryEducation
    1. 2020–2023
      3 years

      Researcher

      Invenia Labs, Cambridge, UK

      Risk-aware decision models under uncertainty for bidding in seven US electricity markets (MISO, PJM, ERCOT, CAISO, SPP, ISO-NE and NYISO). Related research: estimating uncertainty for robust decisions and strategic bidding.

      • Directed projects on daily bidding under price uncertainty, in which machine-learning estimates of that uncertainty define robust and distributionally robust bidding models.
      • Contributed to a detailed market simulator for counterfactual analysis, and led the data analysis of simulation accuracy.
      • Developed decomposition methods for large-scale integer and continuous models, and estimated unknown grid parameters with inverse optimization and heuristics.
      • Explored exact and surrogate methods for bilevel problems such as strategic bidding and hyperparameter tuning, using automatic differentiation and automatic dualization of optimization models.
