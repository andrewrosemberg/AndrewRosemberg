    Software

    Open-source implementations of the research

    Each package implements one of the ideas on the research page and works with optimization models as they are already written in JuMP, so that learning, sensitivity analysis, and models of uncertainty can be applied without rebuilding a model for each method. They are grouped by the idea they implement; within each group, general tools come first and their applications follow. Each package has a page on the design ideas it contributes, with an example and the research it supports.

    Learning solution maps

    Data, training, and supervision for solution maps

    Infrastructure for learning approximations of parametric optimization problems: Generating reference solutions and sensitivities from existing models, training proxies on them, and solving batches of problems on GPUs. See Learning Solution Maps and Continuation-Value Representations.

    LearningToOptimize

    Organization lead · solution-map learning

    An open-source organization for learning optimization proxies, hosting with collaborators:

    • LearningToOptimize.jl: Datasets from parametric JuMP models and proxy training
    • L2OALM.jl, L2ODLL.jl, L2ODC3.jl: Primal–dual, dual, and DC3 learning
    • BatchNLPKernels.jl, BatchConeKernels.jl: GPU kernels for batches of problems
    • Hugging Face datasets: Parametric problems and sensitivity systems

    Policies for multistage decisions

    State policies implemented by optimization, and the models that test them

    Training policies whose state targets are implemented by an optimization over the operational model, and the multistage planning models in which those policies are compared with stochastic dual dynamic programming. See Policies for Multistage Decisions and Planning and Realization Mismatch.

    DecisionRules.jl

    Author · policy learning

    Trains two-stage generalized decision rules: A parametric policy prescribes state targets, an optimization implements them, and the multipliers of the target constraints supply the gradient. Examples cover hydro dispatch and inventory control; DecisionRulesExa.jl solves the stage problems on GPUs.

    HydroPowerModels.jl

    Author · multistage hydrothermal planning

    Multistage hydrothermal dispatch solved with SDDP over any PowerModels network formulation, from transportation models to convex relaxations of AC power flow; used to measure the cost of planning on a simplified network and as an SDDP baseline for decision rules.

    Uncertainty and ambiguity · Multi-level decision problems

    Uncertainty, market response, and solution sensitivity

    A general engine for decisions under estimated uncertainty, its specialization to offers that are evaluated through market clearing, and the sensitivity layer that lets parameters of either be tuned through the decisions they produce. See Uncertainty and Ambiguity, Endogenous Response, and Differentiating Optimization Models.

    PortfolioOpt.jl

    Author · uncertainty, ambiguity, and risk

    Composes forecasts, uncertainty and ambiguity sets, risk measures, and constraints into nominal, robust, and distributionally robust allocation models, each compiled to a tractable reformulation, and compares them in rolling backtests.

    Package pageProject pageGitHub ↗Docs ↗
    specialized to offers in a cleared market

    OptimalBids.jl

    Author · market offers

    Turns a market-clearing model into an evaluation of offers—build the market, change bids, clear, compute profit—so that gradient-based and derivative-free methods search over the same decision process.

    DiffOpt.jl

    Contributor · solution sensitivities

    Part of the JuMP ecosystem: Solution and objective sensitivities of optimization models with respect to named parameters, independent of the solver. Contributions: The module for smooth nonconvex programs at regular local solutions, objective sensitivities, and, with Joaquim Dias Garcia, the parameter-centric interface.

    Differentiating Optimization Models (with DiffOpt.jl)GitHub ↗Docs ↗

    Also: SquidGame.jl, strategy tournaments for repeated games such as the iterated prisoner’s dilemma, written for fun. More on GitHub.
